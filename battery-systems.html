Two technologies

The right chemistry for the job.

LFP batteries are proven and compact, which suits on-site backup and daily load shifting. Flow batteries store energy in liquid electrolyte held in external tanks, which suits long-duration storage.

Lithium iron phosphate (LFP)

Proven storage for sites and microgrids.

  • Stable chemistry with long cycle life
  • Compact enough for homes, shops and commercial sites
  • Backup power for critical loads during outages
  • Energy management to reduce peak demand and utility cost
  • Generates the real load profiles and utility economics the program builds on
Flow batteries

Built for long duration and long life.

  • Power and energy sized separately. The stack sets the power and the tanks set the energy, so adding hours of storage means adding electrolyte, not stacks
  • Water-based electrolyte. Aqueous electrolytes are non-flammable, which supports operation near homes, businesses and critical facilities
  • Abundant materials. Iron chemistry is the primary path, with low-cost materials; vanadium is tested at small scale for comparison

Data & safety

Every system measured. Every system protected.

Utilities already know batteries work. What they want before committing is evidence: proven availability, measured efficiency, known degradation and known maintenance cost.

>98% availability

Microgrid availability target, with every outage logged and explained.

75% round-trip efficiency

Bench flow cell goal, measured on every cycle at the cell and at the AC connection.

≥98% data capture

Monitoring target from day one, with SCADA dashboards and remote access.

18-month record

Daily cycling record goal, with every maintenance event, part and hour on file.

Safety by design
  • Secondary containment for all electrolyte
  • Safety review before any chemistry is purchased
  • Automatic shutdowns verified before unattended cycling
  • Fire and gas detection in enclosed systems
