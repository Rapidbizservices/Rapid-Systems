Electronics Components Sourcing

🌟 Who We Are

RB-Systems is a specialized electronic components sourcing and procurement company serving OEM customers across the United States. We help manufacturers, contract assemblers, and industrial organizations procure obsolete, end-of-life (EOL), allocated, and hard-to-find electronic components from our global network of authorized suppliers and trusted inventory partners.

Our expertise lies in locating critical semiconductors, integrated circuits (ICs), connectors, relays, passives, and other electronic components that are no longer readily available through conventional distribution channels. We work closely with our U.S.-based customers to ensure continuity of supply, minimize production downtime, and support legacy equipment maintenance and manufacturing needs.

🚀 What We Do

  • Source obsolete, end-of-life (EOL), and hard-to-find electronic components from a global supplier network
  • Procure semiconductors, integrated circuits (ICs), connectors, relays, sensors, and passive components
  • Support OEMs and contract manufacturers with shortage mitigation and last-time-buy requirements
  • Coordinate supplier communications, RFQs, pricing negotiations, and delivery schedules
  • Provide component sourcing solutions for legacy equipment maintenance and production continuity
  • Assist with supplier verification, quality documentation, and counterfeit risk mitigation
  • Manage logistics, inventory sourcing, and commercial coordination to ensure on-time delivery
  • Serve as a trusted procurement partner for U.S.-based OEM customers seeking reliable component availability

🧭 Why Choose Us?

RB-Systems specializes in sourcing obsolete, end-of-life (EOL), and hard-to-find electronic components that are critical to maintaining production and supporting legacy equipment. Our extensive global supplier network, industry expertise, and commitment to quality enable us to quickly locate scarce components and deliver reliable sourcing solutions for U.S.-based OEMs.

We help customers reduce downtime, overcome supply chain disruptions, mitigate allocation challenges, and secure authentic components through trusted sourcing channels. By acting as a single point of contact, we simplify the procurement process, improve response times, and ensure consistent support for your ongoing manufacturing and maintenance needs.
