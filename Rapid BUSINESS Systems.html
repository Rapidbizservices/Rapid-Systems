International Industrial Sourcing


🌟 Who We Are

RB-Systems operates as an international sourcing and procurement company serving customers in the United States. We work as a commercial intermediary between U.S. buyers and Indian manufacturers, helping customers identify, evaluate, and procure industrial components and engineered products from India.

🚀 What We Do

  • Source mechanical components from qualified Indian suppliers
  • Facilitate procurement of castings, forgings, and machined parts
  • Coordinate supplier communication, quotations, and sample evaluation
  • Assist with vendor selection and production sourcing
  • Support quality, logistics, and commercial coordination between buyers and suppliers
  • Serve as a local point of contact for sourcing activities in India

🧭 Why India?

India offers a strong ecosystem of engineering, manufacturing, machining, foundry, and industrial suppliers. RB-Systems helps U.S. customers access these capabilities efficiently while reducing the complexity of dealing with multiple suppliers across different regions.

🧭 Pune Operations

RB-Systems is establishing a small operational presence in Pune to support supplier interactions, receive samples and prototype parts, manage business correspondence, and coordinate sourcing activities with manufacturing partners.
